About Us

We nurture brilliance,
and build futures that matter.

Founded in 2000 in Pune, Maharashtra, Prestige Institute of Excellence is affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University and approved by AICTE.

World-Class Faculty

Learn from 200+ accomplished professors, industry veterans, and published researchers committed to mentoring every student.

Why Choose Us

Industry Partnerships

Placement Support

95% placement record with an average package of ₹6.5 LPA. Dedicated career cell, mock interviews, and aptitude training.
