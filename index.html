About Us

Government Approved,
Board for Open learning everywhere.

Dwinethra offers Open learning everywhere to 10th and 12th dropouts, providing a second chance to build their future.

Empowering Underprivileged Learners

Extending educational access to underprivileged learners and school dropouts through open and distance learning for 10th and 12th standard.

Our Values

Our Values

Three pillars that guide Dwinethra: Accessibility of learning, educational Opportunity for all dropouts, and Credibility under government approval.

01. Accessibility
02. Opportunity
03. Credibility

Accessibility

We extend educational access to underprivileged learners and school dropouts, bringing quality schooling directly to those who need it most.

Opportunity

We provide S.C (10th) & S.S.C (12th) dropouts a flexible second chance to qualify, paving the way for higher studies and job opportunities.

Credibility

As a government-approved board listed among India's most trusted educational institutes, our certifications hold high credibility and wide acceptance.
