2. Submit to Google Search Console (Crucial Step)
Google doesn't automatically know your site exists right away. You must tell them:

Go to Google Search Console.
Add a new property and type in your domain: https://httpswwwdwinethra.org/ (or use the Domain verification method).
Verify ownership of the domain. Google usually gives you a TXT record that you will need to add to your DNS Management page.
3. Request Indexing
Once you are inside Google Search Console:

Paste your exact URL https://httpswwwdwinethra.org/ into the top search bar.
It will say "URL is not on Google". Click the Request Indexing button.
This forces Google's bots to crawl your site immediately and read all the "Dwinethra" keywords we just added.
